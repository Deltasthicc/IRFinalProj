# 10.10 Comparison with published findings

The proposal requires a table of our observed ordering against the published one. Corpora differ (BEIR and DPR use Wikipedia and web text, FinanceBench uses questions about public filings, ColPali uses ViDoRe page-image tasks), so we compare the direction of each effect, not absolute numbers.

| Claim | Published (source) | Ours | Same direction? |
|---|---|---|---|
| BM25 is a strong baseline that dense retrievers often fail to beat out of domain | BEIR (Thakur et al. 2021) | Dense is ahead of BM25 overall (nDCG@10 0.782 against 0.699; descriptive, not separately tested). BM25 minus dense on entity needs -0.092 (95% CI -0.208 to +0.019) and on terminology needs -0.091 (CI -0.324 to +0.079): both inconclusive (H2a) | No. We do not find BM25 ahead of dense, though the per-type intervals include 0 |
| Dense retrieval wins where questions paraphrase the text; BM25 wins where exact strings matter | DPR (Karpukhin et al. 2020) | Topical needs: dense minus BM25 +0.068 (CI -0.055 to +0.201; 4 wins, 3 losses), inconclusive (H2b). On entity and terminology needs, where exact strings should favour BM25, dense is nominally ahead (see above) | Partly. The sign for paraphrase-like questions agrees but is inconclusive; BM25 does not win on exact-string needs |
| Fusing lexical and dense rankings helps | RRF (Cormack et al. 2009); BEIR | Hybrid minus BM25 +0.085 (CI +0.019 to +0.155; 13 wins, 4 losses), supported (H3a). Hybrid minus dense +0.002 (CI -0.059 to +0.051), tied (H3b) | Yes against BM25 (the lexical side); no gain over dense alone |
| Cross-encoder reranking improves the top of the ranking | Nogueira and Cho 2019; BEIR | Hybrid + rerank minus hybrid +0.044 (CI -0.012 to +0.113; 9 wins, 4 losses, 7 ties), inconclusive (H4); hybrid + rerank minus BM25 +0.129 (CI +0.052 to +0.213) | Same sign, but the interval includes 0, so not shown here |
| Retrieval improves answers to questions about filings; chunking choices matter | FinanceBench (Islam et al. 2023); Jimeno Yepes et al. 2024 | Role C's RAG study (H8, LLM-rated, 20 questions): best-RAG minus closed-book +0.525 (CI +0.225 to +0.850); BM25-RAG minus closed-book +0.150 (CI -0.250 to +0.550). Table-chunk ablation (H5, all 20 needs): dropping table chunks -0.001 nDCG@10 (CI -0.002 to +0.000), dropping numeric tokens +0.009 (CI -0.009 to +0.030); the table-need subset was not computed | Yes for retrieval that is strong enough (best-RAG), not shown for BM25-RAG. Chunking: no effect found over all needs; the table-need subset was not analysed |
| Page-image retrieval beats text pipelines on visually rich documents | ColPali / ViDoRe (Faysse et al. 2024) | Not evaluated: the vision track is gated (only the MaxSim scoring is implemented and tested; there is no page-image encoder or results file) | Cannot compare |

Where our ordering differs from the published one, the report gives the most likely reasons (domain, query style, corpus size, 20 queries) and does not hide the difference.
